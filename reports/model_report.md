## 7. Monotone constraints and their measured CV effect

Constraint: BonusMalus non-decreasing in the components frequency, tweedie. A worse claims record (higher BonusMalus) should not lower the expected claim frequency or pure premium: the constraint removes non-monotone artefacts of a tree ensemble on this factor. It is frozen in protocol section 5 (frequency Poisson and Tweedie GBMs non-decreasing in BonusMalus; severity Gamma GBM and all other factors unconstrained).

Smallest step of the raw-score sweep (a negative value would be a violation): frequency 0.00e+00; severity -1.57e-02; tweedie 0.00e+00.

CV effect, unconstrained minus constrained (negative = the unconstrained variant is better). optimistic tuning scores of CV folds also used for early stopping and grid scoring; not unbiased estimates and not test results.

## 11. Limits

- Intervals are conditional on the frozen fitted models; retraining, tuning and selection variability are not included. The group-bootstrap CI of Delta is [-2.12406, -0.77571]; the policy-level sensitivity is narrower ([-1.89286, -0.92372]) because it ignores dependence inside rating-profile groups.
- GBM CV scores are optimistic tuning scores (early stopping and grid scoring share the folds).
- The GBM hyperparameter grid was committed before tuning (2 values per axis): 9 of 9 chosen values lie on the lowest or highest grid value, so the optimum may lie outside the grid.
- The selected GBM `gbm_freq_x_sev` is not guaranteed monotone in BonusMalus: 16.35% of the sweep rows show a decrease (largest relative decrease 1.55%); reported, never retuned.
- Test calibration (observed / predicted): selected GBM `gbm_freq_x_sev` 1.0303, selected GLM `glm_freq_x_sev[simplified]` 1.0148; a ratio above 1 means total under-prediction (by 3.03% for the selected GBM). Across the GBM candidates: `gbm_freq_x_sev` 1.0303, `gbm_tweedie` 1.0731. These are descriptive totals on one test fold, not a decision criterion.
- Descriptive only, no decision: on the test fold `glm_tweedie` has a primary loss 74.415 against 74.567 for the selected GLM `glm_freq_x_sev[simplified]` (difference 0.153; lower is better). The GLM was selected on train CV before the test evaluation and is not re-chosen on test.
- GLM interaction add-back is deferred: exploratory and descriptive, train sample only; no GLM interaction is refitted (deferred by the 2026-11-08 amendment); the DrivAge contribution varies with VehPower only through the interactions the trees learned, shared with the VehPower contribution (interpretation.json).
- Loaded premium (flat excess loading): observed uncapped / loaded ranges from 0.8363 to 0.8786 across the 6 candidates, so no candidate's loaded total matches the observed uncapped total.
- Claims are capped at 100 000: 17.71% of the matched cost sits above the cap and enters only through a flat loading, 35.51 per exposure-year, so the loaded premium cannot reflect risk differences in the excess.
- 26.75% of the recorded claims have no severity row; the frequency target counts severity rows (26 444) rather than recorded ClaimNb (36 102).
- 195 orphan severity rows (788 714) are excluded.
- The severity model explains -0.35% of the CV deviance against the constant (a negative value means no gain over the constant).
- Evaluation is on one pre-registered grouped test fold (5 321 claims); the journal lists 1 attempt(s).
